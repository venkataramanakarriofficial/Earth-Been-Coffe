# MASTER WEBSITE DEVELOPMENT PROMPT — EARTH BEEN COFFEE

## ROLE & OBJECTIVE

Act as a world-class creative director, luxury brand website designer, senior frontend architect, 3D web developer and UI/UX specialist.

Your task is to design and develop a complete, production-quality website for **EARTH BEEN COFFEE**, a London speciality coffee brand.

The final website must feel like a premium, custom-designed digital brand experience — not a generic café template, AI-generated dashboard or basic landing page.

**3D interaction inspiration:**
https://motionsites.ai/3d-websites?prompt=sky-cookie

Use this reference to understand immersive product presentation, visual depth, smooth transitions and sophisticated scroll interactions. Do not copy its branding, assets or implementation.

The client's original brief prioritises editorial design, professional photography, simple navigation, mobile performance and subtle animation. Our proposed 3D experience is a creative enhancement that must respect those requirements and receive client approval.

## 1. PROJECT SCOPE

**Project:** Earth Been Coffee Official Website

**Industry:** Speciality coffee, matcha and premium beverages

**Location/Market:** London, United Kingdom

**Website type:** Premium informational brand website with selective interactive 3D experiences.

**Development scope:** Frontend only.

Do not implement unnecessary backend architecture, databases, authentication, shopping carts, online payments, checkout flows or ordering functionality.

The website should:

- Introduce the Earth Been Coffee brand professionally.
- Showcase speciality coffee and other premium drinks.
- Communicate the company's brand story and values.
- Help customers discover stall locations.
- Support future stalls and seasonal promotions.
- Provide partnership, collaboration and trading enquiry routes.
- Work beautifully on mobile, tablet and desktop.

Use:

Build using clean, reusable components, maintainable architecture and a responsive, accessible interface.

Avoid installing excessive animation libraries or creating unnecessary dependencies.

The project must run locally and be ready for production deployment.

## 4. HOMEPAGE — SIGNATURE 3D EXPERIENCE

The homepage is the most important design deliverable.

Design a high-impact, elegant homepage that creates a memorable first impression.

### 4.1 Opening experience

Create a short, tasteful opening transition.

Concept:

1. The page opens with a refined cream or deep-green visual.
2. The Earth Been Coffee logo appears elegantly.
3. A realistic, branded takeaway coffee cup is introduced through gentle cinematic motion.
4. The opening blends smoothly into the main hero.

Do not create a long loading screen or force visitors to watch a non-skippable animation.

Do not make the opening animation repeat on normal navigation.

Respect the user's reduced-motion preferences.

### 4.2 Main hero section

Build an immersive editorial hero containing:

- The official Earth Been Coffee logo.
- A strong brand headline.
- Short, elegant supporting text.
- A featured 3D coffee cup or similarly suitable beverage asset.
- Clear navigation to the menu and brand story.
- A subtle scroll indicator.

**Proposed creative concept:**

A high-quality Earth Been Coffee cup rests or floats within a carefully lit studio-style environment. The cup gently rotates and responds subtly to scroll progression. A few coffee beans or soft shadows may provide depth.

The hero background should remain clean and uncluttered.

The headline should look beautiful on desktop and remain highly readable on mobile.

Do not invent official brand slogans. Use approved copy from the brand deck or clearly marked provisional copy.

### 4.3 Scroll-controlled 3D storytelling

As visitors scroll:

- The featured cup may tilt or rotate gently.
- The camera may shift slightly to reveal depth.
- The scene transitions into real drink photography.
- Brand copy appears through subtle scroll reveals.
- The visual language remains coherent.

Avoid full-page scroll hijacking or overly complicated 3D scenes.

Use 3D where it improves the experience, not simply to demonstrate technical skill.

### 4.4 Brand introduction

Create an editorial section introducing Earth Been Coffee.

Use concise, compelling storytelling based on the client's supplied brand deck.

Suggested layout:
- Large elegant typography
- Carefully arranged professional imagery
- A short story excerpt
- A clear link to the Our Story page

### 4.5 Featured drinks

Showcase selected drinks using premium visual compositions.

Possible categories:
- Speciality coffee
- Signature matcha
- Luxury hot chocolate
- Premium teas and chai
- Iced drinks
- Seasonal specials

Use real approved photographs whenever available.

Do not invent product names, prices or ingredient details.

Interactive effects may include subtle image scaling, transitions and hover movement.

### 4.6 Seasonal highlight

Include an optional seasonal-drink feature.

Its content must be easy to replace in the source code.

### 4.7 Find Us preview

Show a visually appealing preview of current stall locations.

Include a link to the complete Find Us page.

### 4.8 Closing section and footer

Create a strong, elegant closing composition with:

- Brand identity
- Menu navigation
- Location navigation
- Instagram/social links
- Business email
- Partnership/contact link
- Appropriate copyright text

Avoid generic newsletter forms unless approved.

- GLB/GLTF asset support

- Controlled lighting and shadows
- Responsive camera configuration

The 3D cup should incorporate the official logo once available.

Provide:
- Smooth transitions

- Scroll-controlled movement
- Loading-state handling
- Static image fallback
- Graceful handling of unsupported devices
- Reduced-motion alternative

Do not let 3D rendering block essential website content.

## 10. CONTENT MANAGEMENT WITHOUT A BACKEND

Store editable content in clearly structured local data files.

Examples:

- `src/data/menu.ts`
- `src/data/locations.ts`
- `src/data/socials.ts`
- `src/data/siteContent.ts`
- `src/data/seasonal.ts`

The client must be able to update menu items, prices, locations, hours and seasonal highlights without changing component logic.

No database or custom admin dashboard is required.

Use realistic design placeholders only where absolutely necessary, and clearly label all unapproved content.
