# Earth Been Coffee — Homepage Creative Direction

**Idea:** *A little earth. A lot of soul.* (provisional, not a claimed official slogan)

The concept pairs the warmth of an editorial coffee magazine with the unexpected depth of a premium product film. Rather than imitate the referenced MotionSites 3D page, the hero uses a brand-colour, simulated paper cup that responds naturally to a visitor's pointer and slow scroll. The opening is short and optional.

**Art direction:** Deep evergreen, warm cream, coffee-bean browns, muted sage and an occasional pale-gold highlight. Angular sans-serif letterforms contrast with quiet, organic italic serif headlines. Generous negative space and deliberate image ratios create a bespoke editorial feel.

**Narrative sequence:** brand/3D first impression → moments-of-everyday story → three featured drink categories → oversized quote statement → London location invitation → large typographic closing.

**Mobile:** brand introduction comes first, cup scrolls beneath it, menu becomes full-screen, and drink categories stack vertically for easy browsing. Accessibility and information are not dependent on the 3D model.

**Client decision needed:** Approve the creative direction and 3D embellishment; supply official brand assets and information before production release.
